<RuleNote label="One accent">
  Pick Spark or Grit by mood and use exactly one per piece — never two as co-headline colours. The
  accent marks the thing that <em>is</em> the payoff, never chrome: a page with Spark on its section
  numbers has none left for its result.
</RuleNote>

<RuleNote label="Ramps">
  Same mix ratio for every colour so the ramps stay visually consistent. 100/300 mix the base 80% /
  40% toward white, 500 is the base hex unchanged, 700/900 mix 35% / 65% toward black. Use 700 for
  hover and pressed states, 900 for a low-opacity wash on black.
</RuleNote>

<SectionHeading seq="05" title="Hero Text" meta="Two-tier lockup" />

<RuleNote>
  The two-tier headline move, pulled from the thumbnail archive. Three rules the real footage follows
  every time. One, a translucent black box carries the text so the photo reads through it faintly.
  Two, both tiers are one heavy face split by colour — one line white, one line the mood accent,
  never two different fonts. Three, the rows are flush edge-to-edge: the first letters align exactly
  and the last letters end exactly together, with zero pixel overhang either side.
</RuleNote>

<RuleNote label="Not yet a component">
  Font size alone cannot produce that flush edge — differing side-bearings mean the two words also
  need a small horizontal nudge before sizing closes the right edge. No component in this library
  implements the measurement yet, so hero lockups are still authored per piece. Treat this section
  as the spec, not as something you can drop in.
</RuleNote>

<SectionHeading seq="06" title="Layout & Composition" />

<ShapeRule />

<RuleNote label="Corners carry metadata">
  Solid black pill, white all-caps text — year, make, series nickname. Placement flexes to the
  photo's negative space. Headlines sit over sky, dirt or dark zones, never across car detail, on a
  full-bleed real photo — never stock. The badge shows only when a human is on camera; omit it for
  pure car-detail shots.
</RuleNote>

<RuleNote label="Never AI-invented subjects">
  AI composes and grades real source photos only. If the source is missing, it asks before
  generating anything. Hard rule.
</RuleNote>

<SocialLayouts />

<RuleNote label="Sized for the platform">
  Each frame is its own real photo, cropped and framed for its own aspect ratio — never one shot
  stretched or padded to fit several formats. Elements are authored against each format's real
  export resolution and expressed in container units, so they hold at any preview size.
</RuleNote>
